## Date, room, and occupancy facts

- Property-local time is India Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata). Operational dates
  such as today, arrivals, departures, and relative dates are interpreted in IST.
- A stay from check-in date A to checkout date B occupies the nights from A
  through the night before B. Checkout day is not an occupied night. A departure
  and a new arrival may use the same room on the same date.
- A unit is available for a stay only when it is free for every requested night.
- Private rooms generally accommodate two guests. The Mountain View Family Room
  accommodates up to four. Every sleep pod accommodates one guest, so a group
  needs one free pod per person.
- Maintenance blocks make units unavailable. Cancelled bookings do not occupy
  inventory.
- Unpaid soft holds are pending and do not block inventory.
- Nami's `checked_in` status has been maintained inconsistently. Room-grid and
  stay-date coverage are the reliable basis for determining who is staying on a
  given night.

## Pricing and payment facts

- Owner-set FIXED prices live in the core (rooms: suite ₹9,500, premium
  ₹6,500, terrace ₹4,000, sleep pod ₹1,200 per night; pottery ₹1,500; morning movement ₹500; Tapas circuit,

  availability is always checked live.
- Current prices and tax treatment vary by date, booking channel, party size,
  and meal plan. Historical or indicative website prices are not current quotes, except the owner-set fixed prices in core.
- Booking channels include direct website booking, front desk/PMS, Airbnb,
  Booking.com, Agoda, and Hostelworld. The same room and night may have different
  prices by channel.
- Meal plans may include room only, breakfast, breakfast plus one meal, and full
  board. Prices may vary with party size.
- Live booking data provides base amount, tax, and total including tax. Tax comes
  from the current booking response rather than a historical approximation.
- Total including tax is the bill. Amount paid includes recorded online and cash
  payments. Balance is total including tax minus amount paid; a negative balance
  represents credit.
- A quote is not a booking or hold. Inventory is reserved only when the current
  operational system confirms it.
- A booking voucher URL is accessible to anyone possessing it and may reveal
  guest-facing booking information.
