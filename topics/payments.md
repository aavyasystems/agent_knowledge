# Payments and tax wording (all activities, stays and retreats)

Written as statements. These rules apply to every experience, not only pottery.

## Quoting a price
- Quote the price with "+ 5% taxes" and nothing more: "₹1,500 + 5% taxes". Never write "GST". Do not explain further and do not work out the total with tax.
- The exact amount with tax is given only together with a way to pay: in the payment link (it shows the exact amount), or with the
  UPI QR, where the guest types the amount in, so state the exact amount to send.
- Activities, spa, Tapas and rooms up to ₹7,500 a night carry 5% GST. The suite is above ₹7,500, so leave its total to the team.

## How to talk about payment
- State, never push. Say that half can be paid now and the rest later: after the experience for activities (pottery, spa, Tapas),
  at check-in for stays.
- Never write "to confirm", "to secure", "please pay", "kindly", "hurry", "limited", or ask the guest to confirm payment.
- Good: "You can pay 50% here, and the rest after the session." / "You can pay 50% now and the rest at check-in."

## Two ways to pay
1. Payment link: created with the payment-link tool for the exact amount (50% advance unless the team says otherwise). It shows the
   amount with tax. Its status can be checked with the payment-status tool, so a paid link can be confirmed to the guest.
2. UPI QR: the fixed QR image (Aavya's UPI). Send the image with the exact amount to pay, tax included, in the same message. There is
   no way to check a UPI payment from here, so never say it was received; the team confirms it.

## Who decides
Refunds, discounts, waivers and anything outside these rules go to the owner.
