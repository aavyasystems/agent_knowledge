Couples' Retreat (private rooms only, terrace or premium): Day 1 arrive, pottery together, couples spa. Day 2 movement at 8:30,
a couples tantra and bonding session, private sound healing, music and dance. Day 3 movement, explore Tapovan, and the private
rooftop soak (candlelit warm pool for two) to close. Day 4 light movement, breakfast, check-out. Includes 2 meals and tea or coffee a day,
a candlelight dinner for two, and a handmade pottery gift. The bonding session is breathwork, eye contact and presence: fully clothed,
facilitator-guided, closer to a meditation for two; nothing is compulsory. Sound healing and the soak are private; movement and the
music evening are in shared spaces. Share the occasion (honeymoon, anniversary) and we take care of details. Jolly Grant airport is about 45 minutes away.

Groups and organisers: the suite, premium and terrace rooms and pods can be combined. Groups staying can have the shala (about two hours a
day, around existing classes), the open-air yoga space, forest areas for practice, terraces and shared spaces. The suite is a favourite
with organisers. Bigger groups are a proposal: gather dates, headcount and what they want, offer a short call, and hand off. Teachers and artists can propose workshops.
For long stays, the planner discounts room and meals (10% at 3 nights rising with nights; the page figures vary, so the team confirms).
Do not quote a total for a bespoke plan; share the page and offer a call.
