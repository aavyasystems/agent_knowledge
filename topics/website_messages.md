# Reading website messages and openers

Many chats start with a pre-filled message from aavya-rise.com. Read the reference code, answer what they want, and attach the one relevant link.

| Message starts with | Meaning | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I'm interested in a stay" (HOME-WEB), with When and People | Room enquiry | Check availability, quote fixed prices, ask nights only if missing. aavya-rise.com/stay |
| "I'd like to book Tapas" (TAPAS-WEB), session and day | Tapas or sauna request | Confirm option and price, ask arrival time, hand off to confirm. aavya-rise.com/tapas |
| "I'd like to join the pottery studio" (POT-WEB) | Pottery | Price, hours, ask time and numbers. aavya-rise.com/pottery |
| "I'd like to book the recording studio" (STUDIO-WEB) | Studio | Collect format, length, day. aavya-rise.com/recording-studio |
| "I'd like to book at Aavya Sauna & Spa" | Spa | Ask day and time. aavya-rise.com/spa |
| "I'm interested in <retreat>" (RET-BEST, RET-FIREICE, RET-WEB) | Retreat | Ask dates and who is coming, offer a call. aavya-rise.com/retreats |
| "plan my day" or "custom retreat" with a list and an estimate | Planner | Treat the total as an estimate, check availability, hand off. |
| "I'd like to reserve <event> on <date>" | Schedule event | Check against the schedule; team books. aavya-rise.com/schedule |
| "I'd like to come to <event>" with a third-party link | Listing referral | Treat as an event enquiry. |
| "Get Your Booking Voucher" / "Get Your Stay Location" | Automated buttons | Location: maps link and approach note. Voucher: team or owner agent. |
| "I saw your pottery ad" | Ad enquiry | Pottery basics and ask the date. |
| "Can I get more info on this?" | Shared post or ad | Ask which experience or stay they saw. |

Openers: a short greeting at most, then what Aavya is in one line, then ask stay, day visit or experience, with dates and numbers.
Do not reply twice to the same message. Do not repeat a greeting. Match the guest's language.
