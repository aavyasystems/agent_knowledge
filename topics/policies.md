# Policies (as published on aavya-rise.com)

## Cancellation and rescheduling, rooms and retreats (policy page updated April 2026)
- 21 days or more before check-in: 25% of the booking value is retained.
- 10 to 21 days before: 40% retained.
- Less than 10 days before: 50% retained (equal to the full advance).
- Last-minute cancellation or no-show: no refund.
- One reschedule is allowed, subject to availability, at least 10 days before check-in. Revised bookings may show updated pricing.
The older Terms page (February 2026) lists 50% retained up to 72 hours and 100% inside it. Use the April page, and let the owner decide any exception.

## Day retreats
Confirmed with 100% advance. Cancel 24 hours or more before: 75% refund. Under 24 hours: 50% refund. No-show: no refund.
"We understand that plans can change. Where possible, we aim to be supportive."

## Payment
- 50% advance confirms a room booking; the balance is due at or before check-in. A booking is confirmed only after the advance.
- Pay in full for an extra 5% off a custom retreat (mention it to the team).
- Routes: payment link, UPI QR, card link for international guests, Wise to a UPI ID. Aavya does not take PayPal. The team shares payment details; never put payment IDs in the chat yourself.
- A voucher page's "Pay now" button can show a different amount from the agreed one; the team says which to pay.

## Tax
Rooms up to ₹7,500 a night carry 5% taxes; above ₹7,500 a night, 18%. Activities, spa and Tapas: 5% taxes. Café bills follow applicable norms.
Prices are quoted before tax.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM (website terms page; the booking engine still shows 1 pm and 11 am, internal only).
Check-in until 11 pm. Early check-in or late check-out is never promised: say "Let me check and get back to you"; the team decides
(the terms page counts an early check-in before 6 am as a half-day extra).

## House guidelines
A peaceful, creative space. Respect quiet hours and shared areas. No loud music in rooms. No outside visitors in rooms without approval.
No illegal substances. Outside food discouraged in common areas. Guests are responsible for belongings; activities are voluntary.

## Who decides
Refunds, fee waivers, moving from an online travel agency to a direct booking, visa letters and any exception belong to the owner.
Never promise a refund or an exception.
