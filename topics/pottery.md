# Pottery & Art Studio

Come spend an afternoon making something. Try the wheel, paint a pot, or follow an idea of your own.
Solo, with a friend, or with your people. Beginners and travelling creatives are welcome. Advance
booking is recommended but walk-ins are usually fine; the team reserves a slot once it has the day,
time and number of people. Studio opens at 11 am, hourly starts until about 5 pm, daily. Never say
there is no pottery on a day because a schedule list shows none. Pages: aavya-rise.com/pottery,
aavya-rise.com/pottery/courses. Studio lead: Shivani.

All prices per person, + 5% GST, 90 minutes each.

Studio memberships: one week ₹8,000, one month ₹21,000 (a two-hour studio session daily, clay,
a tote bag to personalise, a pot to paint; firing and glazing extra). Pottery shop and Savitri by
Shivani are open without booking. Artists can propose workshops.

Drying, firing and collection:
- Terracotta needs about two days to dry before collection, so a piece cannot leave next morning.
  Unfired pieces are fragile keepsakes, not for food or water. Ceramic must be fired before travel.
- Kiln fires twice a week, Monday and Thursday, when work is ready. Bisque firing ₹800 first piece,
  ₹500 each additional. Glaze firing the same. Courier extra, at cost.
- Pottery can pause on days with a special event.
