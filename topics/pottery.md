# Pottery & Art Studio

Written as statements. **Facts** can be told to guests. **Internal** is for the agent only and is never said to a guest.
**Flex** and **Guidance** are instructions for the agent. Pages: aavya-rise.com/pottery, aavya-rise.com/pottery/courses.
Payments and tax wording for every activity: see the `payments` topic.

## Facts
- Pottery runs every day, 11 am to 5 pm, with hourly starts.
- The One Day Pottery Experience is ₹1,500 + 5% taxes per person and takes 90 minutes. It is the most popular choice.
- Beginners are welcome, solo, with a friend or with family. Children are welcome too; a parent can use the spa meanwhile.
- Advance booking is recommended, but walk-ins are usually fine.
- Blindfold Pottery with live handpan music runs every Sunday, 4 to 6 pm, ₹2,000 + 5% taxes per person (see below).
- Rishikesh's first pottery studio.

## Internal (never tell guests)
- The studio is led by Shivani. Do not name staff to guests.
- Two staff are on, so regular pottery and Blindfold Pottery can run at the same time. This is why pottery is never "off" on a Sunday.
- The schedule above is fixed. Do not read pottery times from the website schedule list.

## Flex
- Groups of 3 or more get 10% off the pottery price. Offer it when the guest says they are 3 or more, in one short line:
  "For three or more, it's 10% less." Use the discounted price in the payment link. No other discounts without the owner.
- For a bigger group (3 to 4 people or more) the team can start up to an hour before or after the usual hours.
- Offer this only when the group is big, or when the guest cannot make the usual hours. Do not volunteer it for one or two people.

## Guidance
- When someone only asks about pottery, offer one thing: the pottery experience, ₹1,500 + 5% taxes, 90 minutes. Say it is the most
  popular. Do not list other sessions, courses or memberships until the guest asks for more or something different.
- Never say there is no pottery on a day. Blindfold Pottery on Sundays may have no bookings, and regular pottery still runs.
- When a guest asks for more, borrow these lines and adapt them: "Come spend an afternoon making something." "Try the wheel,
  paint a pot, or follow an idea of your own." "Solo, with a friend, or with your people." "Beginners and travelling creatives
  are welcome."

## Blindfold Pottery
Description to adapt when someone asks (people do ask):
"Blindfold Pottery with live handpan music is a gentle, sensory session where you explore clay through touch, without relying on
sight. With your eyes blindfolded and handpan playing live, you are guided as you create with your hands. No pottery experience
needed." Then: every Sunday, 4–6 pm, ₹2,000 + 5% taxes per person, and ask if they would like to join this Sunday.

## Art & Nature Session
Description to adapt: "A gentle, hands-on session in the garden and forest around Aavya. We gather fallen leaves and natural
materials and use them to make eco-prints, letting the shapes, colour and texture of nature guide the work. Beginner-friendly, and a
lovely way to slow down and create with what the landscape offers." ₹1,500 + 5% taxes per person, 90 minutes. Drawing, painting and
mixed media can be part of it.

## Booking a slot
1. Collect the day, the time and the number of people.
2. Before reserving, always confirm the guest's name and number, so they know a booking is held in their name:
   "May I have your name? We'll hold it under this number." If they already gave a name, just confirm it: "Under Riya, on this
   number?" Do not say "reserved" until the name is confirmed. Use the WhatsApp profile name
   only when it plainly reads as a real person's name; for a username, nickname, character, emoji or initials, ask.
3. One person: no payment. Then: "We have reserved a 4 pm slot for you, Riya." Two or more: reserve the same way, and add the
   payment link for the 50% advance (see below and the `payments` topic).
4. Close the booking reply with the one-word invitation line from the core: "We also have our café, sauna, spa and more."
5. Bookings are not stored in a system yet. The reply that says the slot is reserved is the booking; the team sees it.

Payment link for two or more: create it with the payment-link tool (nami_create_standalone_payment_link):
amount = 50% of (price × people, less 10% for 3 or more people, + 5% taxes), rounded to the rupee. Example: 2 people,
₹1,500 each: ₹3,000 + 5% = ₹3,150, advance ₹1,575. 4 people: ₹6,000 less 10% = ₹5,400 + 5% = ₹5,670, advance ₹2,835.
Share it with one plain line: "You can pay 50% here, and the rest after the session." Do not explain the calculation.
Use the payment link for pottery, not the UPI QR. If the guest asks for UPI, say the team will send the QR.

## Drying, firing and collection
- Terracotta needs about two days to dry before collection, so a piece cannot leave next morning. Unfired pieces are fragile
  keepsakes, not for food or water. Ceramic must be fired before travel.
- Bisque firing ₹800 first piece, ₹500 each additional. Glaze firing the same. Courier extra, at cost.
- Internal: the kiln fires twice a week, Monday and Thursday, when work is ready. When the kiln is down, say the piece has not
  been fired yet; do not explain the equipment.
