# Recording studio (Aavya Creative Studio)

A semi-professional, acoustically treated audio and video studio in Upper Tapovan. Not fully soundproofed, but quiet and
good enough for high-quality educational and creative recordings. Made for yoga teachers, meditation guides, musicians,
mantra artists, healers, coaches, podcasters and retreat leaders. Page: aavya-rise.com/recording-studio.
Prices + 5% GST.

| Format | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Audio recording (music, voice, guided meditations, mantra, voiceover), per hour | ₹1,200 |
| Podcast & video sessions (full audio and video, interviews, talks), per hour | ₹1,800 |

Optional extras: assistant support, a photographer or videographer, post-production editing, podcast concept and strategy,
interview support or a host, and guidance on publishing to YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Price those with the team.
The Aavya Rise Podcast shows the sound quality.

For an enquiry, collect: what they want to record, how long, whether editing or strategy help is needed, preferred day and time.
Same-day visitors: ask the arrival time so the studio is ready. The team confirms the slot.
