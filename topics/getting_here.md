# Getting here

Aavya has always been a little difficult to find: up in the hills of Upper Tapovan, off a road that does not announce itself.
Full directions with the driver's script: aavya-rise.com/find-us. Address: Aavya Creative Wellness Retreats, Balaknath Road
(behind The Ganesha Palace), near Prakriti Café, Upper Tapovan, Rishikesh, Uttarakhand 249192. Nearest landmark: Ganesha Palace.

By car or cab (share this with the driver before the journey): "Aavya Creative Wellness Retreats, Upper Tapovan. Turn left at 60s Café
onto Secret Waterfall Road, stop at the small bridge near Aarcana Café."
- Cars must use Secret Waterfall Road, not Balaknath Road, which narrows sharply and gets vehicles stuck.
- Drive north on Badrinath Road (NH 58) on the west bank of the Ganga; no need to cross Laxman Jhula.
- 60s Café is on the left. Turn left immediately, uphill. Pass Deecon Valley on the way and do not enter it; keep climbing.
- Stop at the small bridge, 25 metres before Aarcana Café (Rock Café on the right). Cars cannot go further.
- Call us. The team walks down in about three minutes and walks you up. There is no big gate; look up the hillside for the pottery studio.

On foot or scooter: turn right at AYM Yoga Shala up Balaknath Road; about 25 metres past Prakriti, a small left uphill is us.
Lost? Send a location on WhatsApp and the team will come to you. If a guest says the road is blocked or damaged, hand off at once;
the team may redirect via Balaknath Road and meet them at the yoga school. Never state road status yourself.

Distances: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun about 35 km, 45–60 minutes by cab (pre-paid taxi counter, Ola or Uber). Rishikesh railway
station about 18 km, 30 minutes. Haridwar Junction about 40 km, about an hour. Munni Ki Reti about 20 minutes by car. Nirvana Yoga
School about a five-minute walk. Tapovan is safe and pleasant to walk. In monsoon fly into Dehradun or take the train rather than driving from Delhi.
Parking is about 125 metres away and the team helps with luggage.

Taxis and transfers: arranged by the team on request. No fare, provider or airport tariff is on record, so never quote one.
Collect pickup point, date, time, passengers and luggage, then hand off. Pickups from Tapovan cafés have been arranged. For a late
flight after check-out, bags can be stored and a taxi arranged; give the pick-up point (the bridge near Aarcana Café) once the team confirms.
Guests arriving by bus (for example at Nepali Farm) or auto: ask where they are and hand off so the team can guide them.
When a guest taps "Get Your Stay Location", send the maps link and the approach note.
