# Rooms and stay

Written as statements. **Facts** can be told to guests. **Internal** is for the agent only. **Guidance** says how to answer.
Pages: aavya-rise.com/stay (one room: aavya-rise.com/stay#<id>). Gallery: aavya-rise.com/gallery.

## Facts
- Private rooms at the edge of the forest, no two alike: restored wood, natural light, a view of mountains, forest or sky.
  Every room has air conditioning and heating, a rainfall shower and strong Wi-Fi. Toiletries provided.
- Plus the Jungle View Sleep Pods: six individual beds for solo travellers or friends.
- Check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM (as on the website's terms page).
- Meals are not included with a room; the café is open to everyone (see `cafe`).
- Pets and children are welcome.
- Parking is about 125 metres away and the team helps with luggage.
- 50% advance confirms a booking; the rest at check-in (see `payments` and `policies`).

## Internal (never tell guests)
- The owner counts six private rooms; the booking system lists the seven above. Offer only what live availability returns.
- Mountain Terrace Studio beds are unresolved (website: twin; one staff reply: king). Do not state the bed type; say the team
  will confirm if asked.
- Never talk about stairs or step counts.

## Guidance
- Always check live availability for every night asked before naming rooms. Offer only rooms free for the whole stay, each on
  its own line with its price and link. Never ask "which room" without the list.
- A stay is one room for all nights. Never split a stay across two rooms. If no room is free for every night, say so and offer
  the nearest dates that are free.
- Two people: the premium and terrace rooms. Three or four people: always the Mountain View Family Room (suite).
- Rooms are for two. A child can join if they can sleep comfortably in the bed with the parents; say it that way:
  "Our rooms are for two, and a little one is welcome if they can share the bed comfortably." Never offer a third adult in a
  room; for three adults, the suite.
- A couple with a child: do not offer Jungle Hideaway (its bed is on a raised platform).
- Pods: for solo travellers or friends who want a bed each. Never offer pods to a couple or a family.
- Early check-in or late check-out: never promise. Say "Let me check and get back to you." The team decides.
- Choosing between rooms: Jungle Hideaway is greener and quieter but has no direct sun; Sunrise Sky has the sunrise and the
  mountains, and is the better winter choice. Hillside and Sunrise Sky have the widest views.
- Three nights is what we suggest to feel Tapovan; there is no minimum stay.
- Extending a stay depends on live availability; if the room is full, the team will look at it.
- Month-long stays are priced by the owner.
