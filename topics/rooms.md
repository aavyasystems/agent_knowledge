# Rooms and stay

Eight places to sleep, no two alike: restored wood, natural light, a view of mountains, forest or
sky. All rooms have air conditioning and heating, a rainfall shower, and strong WiFi throughout.
Fixed nightly prices are in core (suite ₹9,500, premium ₹6,500, terrace ₹4,000, pod ₹1,200).
Photos and detail: aavya-rise.com/stay. Gallery and videos: aavya-rise.com/gallery.

Choosing: between Jungle Hideaway and Sunrise Sky, Hideaway is prettier and greener but gets no direct sun;
Sunrise Sky has the sunrise, mountains and the mango tree, and is the better winter choice. Premium rooms
(Hillside, Sunrise Sky) have the strongest views; terrace studios are small and lovely with outdoor space.
For seniors or couples wanting one bed, offer a private room, not a pod. Two guests in pods need two beds.

Good to know:
- Toiletries provided. Housekeeping on request, daily if wanted. Extra sheets or blankets: ask the team and it is done.
- Parking is about 125 metres from the property and the team helps with luggage.
- No minimum stay; three nights is what the team recommends to feel Tapovan. The stay planner
  (aavya-rise.com/design-your-retreat) discounts room and meals for 3+ nights; the team confirms.
- Check-in is in the afternoon (1 pm in the booking engine, 2 pm on the terms page) and check-out is
  at 11 am (10 am on the terms page). The team says timing is "a little flexible". Early check-in is
  possible when the room was free the night before; a very early arrival can leave bags and freshen up.
  Check-in is allowed until 11 pm. Luggage can be stored after check-out and a later taxi arranged.
- Meals are not included with a room. Add them from the café (see `cafe`) or the planner meal plans.
- Pets are welcome. There is a forest trail behind the property and a house dog; a pet retreat has been hosted.
- Children are welcome. Aavya is uphill in a forest; the number of stairs at each room is not recorded,
  so for mobility questions hand off to the team.
- Booking directly on the website is welcome. 50% advance confirms a booking; see `policies`.
- Extending a stay depends on live availability. If the unit is full, hand off.
- Stays of a month or more are rare and priced case by case by the owner.
- Guests staying with us get 10% off eligible day-retreat sessions (not café meals).
