# Day retreats

Come up for a few hours. Sweat, make something, eat well, go home lighter. No room required.
Page and day planner: aavya-rise.com/day-retreats. Prices per person + 5% taxes.

A day retreat suits travellers with a day in Rishikesh, friends and couples looking for something beyond rafting,
and anyone who wants to slow down without booking a room. Most people stay two to six hours.

Pick from: Tapas sauna and ice circuit (from ₹1,200), guided pottery ₹1,500, a massage, sound healing, a movement class,
then the café. Ready-made: Sauna + 60-min massage ₹3,499 and Sauna & Spa Half-Day ₹4,499 (10:30 am to 6:30 pm).
Add a cooking class, Reiki training or a nearby excursion. Own-pace circuit 8 am to 2 pm and 6 to 9 pm; guided 4 to 6 pm;
spa by appointment 10:30 am to 6:30 pm; pottery hourly 11 to 5; movement 8:30 am.

Sample day: movement ₹500, pottery ₹1,500, guided Fire & Ice ₹2,500, lunch at the café.

Discounts as published: booking a spa therapy gives 10% off Tapas circuit items the same day; staying guests get a further 10% off
eligible sessions after that. Packages and café food are not discounted. Anything beyond this is the owner's decision.

Booking: the website planner builds a day and sends it on WhatsApp with an estimate; the team confirms availability before it is booked.
Spa therapies are by appointment. Bring comfortable clothes, swimwear or shorts, a water bottle. Towels, yoga mats and pottery materials are provided.
Day visitors can add a café meal; one is included in the Half-Day. Custom day retreats are priced per proposal.
