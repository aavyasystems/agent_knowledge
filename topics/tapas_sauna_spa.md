# Tapas Fire & Ice, sauna and spa

Rishikesh's first contrast therapy circuit. A deodar cedar sauna with volcanic stones open to the forest,
a soaking pool, an ice bath at about 5 to 8 degrees (the team has said 6–12), and an open-air movement zone.
"Movement, then sauna, then ice. Then again." Tapas is Sanskrit for heat, the fire of discipline; Tapovan is the
forest of tapas. Pages: aavya-rise.com/tapas, aavya-rise.com/spa. Prices per person, + 5% taxes.

Hours:
- Sauna and own-pace circuit: 8 am to 2 pm and 6 to 9 pm. Guided Fire & Ice: daily 4 pm, 120 minutes.
- Therapies: first appointment 10:30 am, last starts 6:30 pm, prior appointment recommended.
- A sunrise "Surya" circuit (Surya Namaskar, breath and ice, no sauna) is coming soon.
- Guided sessions occasionally pause for a day or two; the team confirms. Walk-ins depend on capacity; the sauna seats five.
- We heat the sauna ahead, so always ask the arrival time. Walking in cold has gone wrong before.

The ritual: Move (bars, rings, mobility), Sauna (15 minutes), Soak (float in open air), Ice (breathe through the first
reaction, stay calm), repeat. Two rounds is the circuit. "No medal for a third." Rest at the end.
Guided: a facilitator leads movement and breath, then sauna, soak and ice, twice. Made for first-timers.
Bring swimwear or shorts and a water bottle. Towels provided. Day visitors welcome every day.
Whether the sauna is mixed, and clothing rules, are not recorded; ask the team.
Medical: pregnancy, heart condition, high or low blood pressure, recent surgery or any concern: check with a doctor and tell the team. Never clear a guest.
