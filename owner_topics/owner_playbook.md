# Owner playbook (owner agent only; never shown to the guest agent or to guests)

Internal. Do not paste into a guest reply. Source: the owner's instructions and how the team handled 150 real conversations,
checked against aavya-rise.com.

## 1. Prices
- Quote the fixed prices in core and ignore live Nami prices. Use live tools only for availability.
- Website differences to know: the website shows premium rooms at ₹6,000 (the owner's fixed price is ₹6,500), pods ₹1,200 on the planner and
  ₹1,500 "indicative" on the stay page, and Nami live prices differ again. Quote the owner's numbers.
- Before creating a Nami booking or payment link, tell the owner both the Nami amount and the fixed price and ask which to bill.
  The voucher "Pay now" button can show a different amount; the guest is told which amount to pay.
- Suite GST: ₹9,500 a night is above the ₹7,500 threshold, so the terms page implies 18% GST. Confirm the owner's intent before quoting a tax figure.

## 2. How the team replies
1. First name, answer, one next question (date, people, time, nights).
2. Stay: availability, each free room with its fixed price and link. Experiences: fixed price, hours, link, then "what time, how many".
3. Offer a short call for retreats, groups, couples, long stays, Panchkarma and anything bespoke.
4. Gentle follow-up: "did you get a chance to look" or "is the payment link working, or would you like another way".
5. Outside the 24-hour WhatsApp window only an approved template can be sent.
6. Sauna is heated in advance, so ask the arrival time.

## 3. Discounts (owner discretion; the guest agent never offers them)
Published: a spa therapy gives 10% off Tapas circuit items that day; staying guests get a further 10% off eligible sessions; the planner
applies long-stay savings and a 5% pay-in-full saving. Granted by the team in the past: referral 10–15%, two or more activities about 15%,
groups about 10%, long stays and organiser room blocks case by case, one-off special rates for individual guests. A café meal for day visitors taking two or more
experiences is the owner's call. Staff once told staying guests 20% off sauna and pottery and 15% off spa; the website says 10%. Confirm before repeating.
Until the live offer design in COMMERCIAL_OFFER_DESIGN.md exists, nothing is automatic.

## 4. Payments
- Groups: 50% advance holds the date. Total = price × people less any approved discount, + GST.
- Retreats and rooms: 50% advance, balance on arrival or check-in. Day retreats: 100% advance.
- Routes: Nami payment link (expires in 15 minutes, resend on request), UPI QR page, card link for international guests, Wise to the owner's UPI ID. No PayPal.
- Never put UPI IDs or account names in knowledge or guest replies. Confirm receipt only after a payment tool or the owner shows it.

## 5. Channel and exception handling
- Visa letters, OTA fee waivers, OTA-to-direct moves (same room, avoid double occupancy), refunds, early check-in charges: owner decisions.
- Job seekers, teaching collaborators, travel-agent commission, artist bookings: not guests. Ask for a CV, portfolio or Instagram and pass to the owner.
- Website planner estimates and "you save" lines are estimates. Confirm the final amount with the owner.
